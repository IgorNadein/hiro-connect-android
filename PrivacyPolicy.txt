HI-RO Connect Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

HI-RO Connect does not contain advertising, analytics SDKs, or third-party push notification services. Account settings, contacts selected by the user, call history, messages, and diagnostic data are stored on the Android device.

The app exchanges SIP signaling, messages, and call media only with servers configured by the user or the organization operating HI-RO Phone Gateway. The operator of that infrastructure may process and retain communications under its own policy.

If Android contacts integration is enabled, HI-RO Connect reads contacts to resolve names and phone numbers. It does not upload the address book to an unrelated third party.

Uninstalling the app removes its private on-device data. Files explicitly exported or backed up by the user remain in the selected destination.
